0 · The cell cycle (the page before mitosis)

This page is in your notebook but it didn't come in the photos. Here is the standard version, so you're covered.

G1
G1 phase: the cell grows and does its normal job.
S
S phase: the cell copies its DNA (S = synthesis). Now each chromosome has two identical copies.
G2
G2 phase: the cell keeps growing and gets ready to divide.
M
M phase: mitosis + cytokinesis. The cell divides into two daughter cells.

Interphase = G1 + S + G2: everything before division. Checkpoints are quality controls: the cell checks that the DNA is copied correctly and repairs damage before it continues.

1 · Mitosis

Sep 3

Definition for the test

Mitosis is the process in which the cell's nucleus divides, so that each new cell receives an identical set of chromosomes.

Think of a photocopier: one original in, two identical copies out.

The 4 phases, in order: P · M · A · T

Please Make Another Twin → Prophase · Metaphase · Anaphase · Telophase

PhaseWhat happensMemory trick
1 · ProphaseChromosomes condense and become visible. The nuclear envelope dissolves. Spindle fibers begin to form.Prophase = Prepare
2 · MetaphaseChromosomes line up in the middle (the equatorial plane). Spindle fibers attach to the centromeres.Metaphase = Middle
3 · AnaphaseSister chromatids are pulled apart to opposite poles. Each chromatid is now an individual chromosome.Anaphase = Away
4 · TelophaseChromosomes decondense into chromatin. Nuclear envelopes reform around two nuclei.Telophase = Two
Prophase and telophase are opposites: in prophase chromosomes condense and the envelope dissolves; in telophase chromosomes decondense and the envelope reforms. Like rewinding a video.

Key vocabulary

Chromosome

A package of DNA. After the DNA is copied (S phase), it looks like an X.

Sister chromatids

The two identical halves of that X.

Centromere

The point in the middle of the X that holds the two chromatids together.

Spindle fibers

Threads made of microtubules that pull the chromosomes, like ropes.

Chromatin

DNA in its loose, relaxed form, like a ball of tangled thread.

Equatorial plane

The imaginary line across the middle of the cell.

2 · Cytokinesis

The cytoplasm divides as the cell membrane pinches inwards, until the cell separates into two daughter cells. Each one gets organelles and a nucleus.

Mitosis ≠ cytokinesis. Mitosis divides the nucleus. Cytokinesis divides the cytoplasm (the rest of the cell). Picture squeezing a balloon in the middle until it becomes two.

3 · Sexual vs. asexual reproduction

Sep 24

ParentsOneTwo
GametesNo fusion of gametesFusion of gametes (egg + sperm)
OffspringGenetically identical (clones)Genetic variation
ExamplesBacteria, amoeba, hydra, yeast, starfishHumans, dogs, birds, sharks

Gametes are the special cells for sexual reproduction in animals, plants and fungi. The egg is the female gamete and the sperm is the male gamete.

Careful with starfish, hydra and yeast: they can reproduce in both ways. Starfish reproduce mostly sexually, but they can also regrow from a piece (fragmentation).

4 · The fertilization process

Oct 1
Gametes→Fertilization→Zygote (2n)→Genetic variation
1
Gametes: separate male and female gametes.
2
Fertilization: the precise moment the gametes fuse.
3
Zygote: the first cell of the new organism. It is 2n: two sets of chromosomes, one set from each parent.
4
Genetic variation: the offspring inherits a unique mix of traits from both parents.

That's why you look a bit like your mom and a bit like your dad, but you are not a copy of either one.

5 · Asexual reproduction in depth

Oct 21

It needs no mate, uses very little energy, and it is nature's ultimate mechanism for rapid multiplication.

✔ Strategic advantage

In a perfectly stable environment, making exact clones is the fastest way to dominate an ecosystem.

✘ Strategic flaw

Zero genetic variation: total vulnerability to new diseases or environmental changes. If one clone gets sick, they all can.

Four ways to build a clone

MethodHow it worksExamples
Binary fissionThe cell splits into two equal halves.Bacteria, amoeba
BuddingA small bud grows on the parent and then breaks off.Yeast, hydra
FragmentationThe body breaks into pieces and each piece grows into a new organism.Starfish, planaria
Spore formationIt releases tiny spores that grow into new organisms.Fungi

Trick: Binary = Break in two · Budding = Baby bump · Fragmentation = Fragments · Spores = Specks in the air.

6 · Mendelian genetics & Punnett squares

Oct 21
Genetics

The study of genes and how they are inherited.

Gene

A section of DNA that codes for a trait.

Allele

A version of a gene: B (brown) or b (blue).

Genotype

The combination of alleles: BB, Bb or bb.

Phenotype

The physical trait you can see: brown eyes. Different genotypes can give the same phenotype.

Gregor Mendel

Grew plants, found patterns and concluded that there are dominant and recessive traits.

Dominant vs. recessive

Capital letter = dominant (B = brown). With one copy it shows.

Small letter = recessive (b = blue). It only shows with two copies: bb.

Homozygous = two equal alleles (BB or bb) · Heterozygous = two different alleles (Bb).

Example 1 · Emilia (Bb) × Peter (Bb)

P \ EBb

Brown eyes: 3/4 (75%) · Blue eyes: 1/4 (25%). Yes, two brown-eyed parents can have a blue-eyed child.

Example 2 · Solving it backwards (Rachel's family)

Rachel has blue eyes, her brother Isaac has brown eyes and their mother has blue eyes. What color are the father's eyes?

1
Rachel is blue → recessive → bb.
2
Mom is blue → bb. She can only give b.
3
Isaac is brown, but he got a b from Mom → he is Bb. His B must come from Dad.
4
Rachel is bb, so Dad also gave her a b.
✔
Dad is Bb, with brown eyes. (Your notebook stops before writing this answer.)

8 · Practice before the test

Answer in your head, then tap the question to see the answer.

What is the correct order of the phases of mitosis?

Prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase (PMAT).

In which phase do sister chromatids move to opposite poles?

Anaphase (A = away).

What does cytokinesis divide?

The cytoplasm. Mitosis divides the nucleus.

Why is sexual reproduction called "the diversity engine"?

Because it mixes genes from two parents and produces genetic variation.

What is a zygote?

The first cell of a new organism, formed when two gametes fuse (2n).

A hydra grows a small bud that breaks off and becomes a new hydra. Which method is it?

Budding.

What is the main weakness of asexual reproduction?

Zero genetic variation: one new disease can affect all the clones.

Two parents are Bb × bb. What fraction of the children have blue eyes?

1/2: the square gives Bb, Bb, bb, bb.

A brown-eyed person has a blue-eyed child. Can the brown-eyed parent be BB?

No. A BB parent always passes B, and the child would be brown. The parent must be Bb.
