A · Mitosis and cytokinesis

1What is the main result of mitosis?

Mitosis divides the nucleus so that each new cell gets an identical set of chromosomes.

2What is the correct order of the phases of mitosis?

PMAT: Please Make Another Twin.

3In which phase do chromosomes condense and the nuclear envelope dissolve?

Prophase = prepare: chromosomes condense, the envelope dissolves and spindle fibers begin to form.

4In which phase do chromosomes line up at the equatorial plane?

Metaphase = middle.

5In which phase are sister chromatids pulled to opposite poles?

Anaphase = away.

6In which phase do nuclear envelopes reform around two nuclei?

Telophase = two: two new nuclei form and chromosomes decondense into chromatin.

7During metaphase, what do the spindle fibers attach to?

The fibers attach to the centromere (at the kinetochore), the point in the middle of each chromosome.

8What are spindle fibers made of?

Spindle fibers are made of microtubules and pull the chromosomes.

9After the sister chromatids separate in anaphase, each chromatid is…

Once separated, each chromatid is considered an individual chromosome.

10Which two phases are like a video played backwards?

Prophase condenses chromosomes and dissolves the envelope; telophase decondenses them and rebuilds it.

11What does cytokinesis divide?

Cytokinesis divides the cytoplasm as the membrane pinches inwards. Mitosis divides the nucleus.

B · Sexual and asexual reproduction

12Why is asexual reproduction called "the cloning machine"?

One parent and no fusion of gametes: the offspring are clones.

13Which of these is a feature of sexual reproduction?

Sexual reproduction joins an egg and a sperm and produces variation.

14Which organism does your notebook list as an example of sexual reproduction?

Your notes list humans, dogs, birds and sharks as sexual, and bacteria, amoeba and hydra as asexual.

15What is the strategic advantage of asexual reproduction?

Clones multiply fast and need no mate, which works well while nothing changes.

16What is the strategic flaw of asexual reproduction?

If all individuals are identical, a disease that kills one can kill them all.

17A bacterium splits into two equal cells. Which method is this?

Binary fission: one cell breaks into two.

18A hydra grows a small bud that breaks off and becomes a new hydra. Which method is this?

Budding: a bud grows on the parent and then separates.

19A planarian is cut into pieces and each piece grows into a new worm. Which method is this?

Fragmentation: each fragment becomes a new organism.

20Which organisms reproduce by spore formation, according to your notes?

Fungi release spores that grow into new organisms.

C · Fertilization

21What are gametes?

Gametes are the cells for sexual reproduction: the egg (female) and the sperm (male).

22What is fertilization?

Fertilization is when the male and female gametes join.

23What is a zygote?

The zygote forms after fertilization and is 2n.

24What is the correct order of the fertilization process?

First the separate gametes, then they fuse, the zygote forms, and the offspring has a unique mix of traits.

25Why are brothers and sisters (who are not twins) different from each other?

Each fertilization combines the parents' genes in a different way: genetic variation.

D · Mendelian genetics and Punnett squares

26What is a gene?

A gene is a section of DNA. The visible version of a trait is the phenotype.

27What is a genotype?

Genotype = the letters. Phenotype = what you see.

28Brown eyes (B) are dominant and blue eyes (b) are recessive. What color are the eyes of a person who is Bb?

With one dominant allele, the dominant trait shows.

29Which genotype is heterozygous?

Heterozygous means two different alleles.

30Two different genotypes, BB and Bb, both give brown eyes. This shows that…

BB and Bb look the same (brown) but have different letters.

31Emilia (Bb) and Peter (Bb) have a child. What is the probability that the child has blue eyes?

The square gives BB, Bb, Bb, bb. Only bb is blue: 1 out of 4.

32Emilia (Bb) × Peter (Bb). What fraction of the children are expected to be heterozygous?

Bb appears in 2 of the 4 boxes: 2/4 = 1/2.

33A Bb parent and a bb parent have children. What fraction is expected to have blue eyes?

The square gives Bb, Bb, bb, bb: half blue.

34A BB parent and a bb parent have children. What fraction is expected to have blue eyes?

The BB parent always passes B, so every child is Bb and brown.

35Rachel has blue eyes, her brother Isaac has brown eyes, and their mother has blue eyes. What is the father's genotype?

Isaac is brown but got a b from Mom, so Dad gave him B. Rachel is bb, so Dad also gave her b. Dad = Bb.

36In Rachel's family, what is Isaac's genotype?

His mother (bb) can only give b. He is brown, so he also has a B: Bb.
